Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: October 2026

The app

3-Level-Books does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking services. Your reading settings and reading position are stored locally on your device only and are removed when you delete the app. In-app purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store; we receive no payment information or personal data from Apple. The app checks whether a book is unlocked via Apple’s StoreKit service; Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. No data is shared with third parties. The app is intended for a general audience and collects no data from users of any age.

The website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data in server logs, such as your IP address, date and time of access, the requested address and your browser’s user agent, to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We do not receive or analyse these logs. Fonts and the video are served from this website itself; there is no connection to Google or other third-party servers. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

Your rights and contact

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For any privacy question, write to paul@gontschar.com.
